Rules & Instructions

Team Size

Each team must have 6 players on the court. You can register up to 8 players total including substitutes.

Match Format

Best of 3 sets. First two sets are played to 25 points, and the final tie-breaker set to 15 points. Win by 2 points.

Uniforms

All teams must wear matching uniforms with visible numbers. Libero must wear a contrasting color.

Code of Conduct

Respect the referees, opponents, and staff. Unsportsmanlike conduct will result in immediate disqualification.
